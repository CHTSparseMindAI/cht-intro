| [Cannistraci-Hebb Training on Ultra-Sparse Spiking Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05581) | ICLR | 2026 | CH-SNN: temporal plus structural sparsity for neuromorphic and edge deployment. |
| [Conversion of Sparse ANN to Sparse SNN Can Save Up to 99% of Energy](https://openreview.net/forum?id=lZrZgZ9wIu) | ICLR | 2026 | Uses CHT-trained sparse ANNs as better sources for SNN conversion. |
