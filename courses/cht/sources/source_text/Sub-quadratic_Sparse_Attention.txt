             Sub-quadratic Sparse Attention
                   Last edited time May 15, 2026 11:37 AM
             Blog: https://subq.ai/how-ssa-makes-long-context-practical
             The SSA (Subquadratic Sparse Attention) architecture introduced by SubQ AI is a
             new long-context transformer approach designed to make million-token AI
             reasoning practical by replacing standard dense attention with dynamically
             selected sparse attention. Instead of comparing every token with every other
             token — which scales quadratically and becomes extremely expensive at long
             context lengths — SSA intelligently retrieves only the most relevant tokens for
             each query, reducing computation toward near-linear complexity while still
             preserving long-range reasoning and retrieval quality. The key innovation is that
             the sparsity is content-aware rather than based on fixed attention patterns,
             allowing the model to efficiently find important information even if it is located
             very far away in the context. According to the reported benchmarks, SSA
             achieves major speedup (x52 speedup for 1M tokens context window) respect to
             Flash Attention while maintaining strong performance on long-context reasoning,
             coding, and retrieval tasks. This architecture could be especially useful for AI
             coding agents analyzing entire repositories, enterprise document intelligence,
             legal and financial analysis over massive datasets, scientific research assistants,
             long-memory conversational agents, and quant research systems where relevant
             information is distributed across extremely large contexts.
